# Entry 6
##### 5/29/26
### Content
In this blog entry, I got a chance to introduce my Freedom Project to my class as well as other visitors. My project was a horror game developed with the use of GDevelop. During the session, I outlined the aim of my project, showed the [game](https://gd.games/games/72bcac7d-88c9-43ec-a761-c179c0c3e44d), and answered questions that were asked of me. Presentation provided an opportunity for me to share all my findings on this project and get some feedback. If you want find out what I [learn](https://github.com/derrickc1170/sep11-freedom-project/blob/main/tool/learning-log.md) from my project and [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5NzMs1JRuXA&list=PL3YlZTdKiS89LrdlHcHo5vePN3O3UhGwm)  that help me learn Gdevelop.

One of the questions that was told to me during the presentation was; how would I be able to improve the game? Indeed, this was a very important question because it pointed out ways through which I can further develop and improve my project. For instance, I could improve this game through adding more levels, making good sound effects, improving the enemy AI, as well as increasing difficulty in the gameplay. Also, Adding the story aspect of the game can be improved to enhance the overall experience of the gamers. Here was my MVP [plan](https://github.com/derrickc1170/sep11-freedom-project/blob/main/prep/plan.md)
### EDP
Currently, I am in the presentation stage of the engineering design process, showing my finished game and receiving feedback. I completed my MVP with core mechanics like a sprint system with stamina, a health bar, a Game Over screen, and a finished map design. My next step in the engineering design process is to use feedback to improve and expand my game beyond MVP.
### Skill 
##### Problem Solving
I demonstrated problem solving by troubleshooting my map, enemy AI, and game mechanics, making adjustments in GDevelop until everything worked correctly.

#### Creativity
I demonstrated creativity by designing a horror-themed game with a full map, enemy AI, a sprint and stamina system, a health bar, and a complete objective system.
